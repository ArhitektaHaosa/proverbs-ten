Proverbs, translations, literal meanings, usage examples, origin notes and modern remixes that can be attributed or are clearly public-domain.

- Spam or promotional content
- Personal data of third parties
- Content that violates copyright without a compatible license
- Hate speech or illegal material

A single human moderator reviews contributions. AI drafts never become public without approval. Thin or unreviewed pages are not indexable.
